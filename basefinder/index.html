World

“Surface” is the biome the ground gets, which is right for bases on the surface. Use a Y level for a base underground or up in the sky; that one includes cave biomes.

Search area

+X is east, +Z is south, same as F3. Everything outside the box is shaded.

Water colour Java edition

Tap the colour the water at the base had. Tap another to add it if you're not sure. Tap again to remove.

Water blends across biome borders, so a base near a border can look in-between: pick both colours then. Bedrock players see different water colours; if you saw it on Bedrock, untick biomes by hand below instead.

Match from the stream

Add a screenshot from the stream and drag a box over the water at the base. Then, from the same stream, mark water in biomes you know the player was in. Those tell the tool how the stream's lighting and colours shift things, and it corrects the base water by the same amount. Best: two or more known waters with different colours, like a river (normal blue) and a swamp or an ocean.

Or paste a screenshot (Ctrl+V) or drop one here. Best samples: open water in daylight, away from the shore, shadows and chat text. Deep water works better than shallow.

Water pictures are drawn shallow (left) to deep (right) in daylight without shaders. They're close, not exact: the real look depends on depth, time of day and the streamer's settings. That's why matching uses the corrected colour, not the picture.

Biomes

The % column is how much of the search area each biome covers.

Possible spots

Each spot is one connected stretch of possible biomes. Tap one to fly there. “Rule out” it once you've checked it.

Ruled out

Places you've already flown over. A radius of 192 is about 12 chunks of render distance.

Check a spot
